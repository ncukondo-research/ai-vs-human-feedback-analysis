EDITORIAL COMMENTS (Decision: B)
-------------------

Thank you for the revision. Please address a few outstanding issues.

Authors noted "the results of the quantitative and qualitative analyses were integrated to gain deeper insights into the differences between AI and supervisor feedback." Could authors provide more information in the manuscript on how those two data sources were integrated together? (This is discussed in the methodological integrity section, but an overview of how this was conducted would be useful earlier on.)

In regards to the qualitative analysis, please provide more information in the manuscript:
— Were codes generated inductively or deductively?
— Were any softwares used to conduct the qualitative analysis?
— How were conflicts in code generation/coding managed?

In the title, abstract, and main text provide the study design (e.g., cross-sectional, etc).
