# TOC Image Plan — JMIR Medical Education submission

Working notes for the Table of Contents (TOC) image accompanying the manuscript
"AI-generated versus human supervisor feedback on medical students' clinical clerkship logs: cross-sectional convergent mixed methods study."

Source of JMIR requirements: <https://support.jmir.org/hc/en-us/articles/115001301407-What-makes-a-good-TOC-image>
(accessed via web.archive.org snapshot dated 2025-01-13; last updated by JMIR Finance Team)

---

## 1. JMIR official requirements

### 1.1 Two production tracks
- **Traditional plain TOC image** — image only, no overlaid text. All guidelines below apply.
- **Visual Abstract** (since 12/2020) — text-bearing graphic produced by JMIR's in-house design service for a fee. Replaces the plain TOC image; design guidelines below do **not** apply.

The default choice is the traditional plain TOC image unless a Visual Abstract is commissioned.

### 1.2 Technical requirements (must)
| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| File format | **PNG only** (JPG rejected because of compression artifacts) |
| Aspect ratio | **4:3** (landscape) — portrait and ultra-wide (e.g. 4:2) prohibited |
| Minimum dimensions | 200 × 150 px (acceptable) |
| Preferred dimensions | at least 800 × 600 px |
| Ideal dimensions | **1200 × 900 px** |
| Color | Color only; black-and-white or monochromatic images not permitted (the JMIR homepage applies a desaturation effect on hover, so B/W images would not behave correctly) |
| Whitespace | No padding/whitespace around the image (this is also why portrait is disallowed) |
| Copyright | Authors must own the copyright, hold a CC-BY/CC-BY-NC/CC0 license, have a purchased stockphoto license (with proof), or use a public-domain image. Source and license must be stated in the upload form. |
| Identifiable persons | If a model's face, tattoos, or other identifying features are visible, signed permission from the model (or legal guardian if <18) is required. |

### 1.3 Content rules (preferences and prohibitions)
- **Preferred:** show *people doing something* relevant to the paper; original work; figure taken from the actual paper if it depicts people using devices, etc.
- **Discouraged but acceptable:** stockphotos (purchased), provided they are not generic clichés (man-at-computer, lone keyboard, stethoscope-on-laptop, etc.).
- **Prohibited:**
  - Logos or text on the image (with narrow exception for JMIR Research Protocols / Formative Research where a "nice" study logo may be allowed; *JMIR Medical Education is not in that exception*).
  - Black-and-white / monochromatic images.
  - Cartoon-style graphics or illustrations (exception: app/video simulation screenshots).
  - Collages (multiple photos grouped).
  - Diagrams, flow charts, bar diagrams.
  - Investigator/author headshots (exception: research-team group photos in JMIR Res Protoc only).
  - Re-using a previously used TOC image, even for the same authors/topic.
  - Screenshots of text-heavy websites (text renders poorly when downscaled). For sister journals a cropped screenshot can work; for *JMIR* main, screenshots should be avoided.
  - Low-quality / overused clip-art.
  - Graphs or charts with anything other than a very large font (essentially text-free).

## 2. What the ideal TOC image looks like for this project

### 2.1 Manuscript context (drives image choice)
- Topic: comparing AI-generated and human supervisor feedback on medical students' clinical clerkship logs.
- Target journal: **JMIR Medical Education** — the *no logos / no text on image* rule applies fully.
- Audience: medical educators; the image should feel relatable and immediately readable as "medical learner receiving feedback."
- Manuscript figures: thematic map and bar/violin plots — none of these are usable as a TOC image (diagrams and charts are explicitly disallowed).

### 2.2 Design brief
A landscape (4:3) color photograph showing a clinical-clerkship learner *doing something* with a written/digital log or feedback — ideally featuring a person. Concretely, any of the following directions satisfy every JMIR rule and stay tightly on-topic:

1. **Student writing or reviewing a clerkship log** at a hospital workstation (laptop/tablet visible, white coat or scrubs, hospital environment). Communicates "clinical clerkship reflection."
2. **Student–supervisor feedback moment** — two people, one clearly a senior clinician, looking together at a log on a screen or printout. Communicates "human supervisor feedback."
3. **Student reading feedback on a screen** with a subtle hint of AI (e.g. chat-style bubbles visible on the screen but not legible). Communicates "AI-generated feedback." Avoid any recognisable LLM/product logo.

A composition that suggests *both* AI and human feedback within a single frame (e.g. student at desk with a printed log on one side and a screen showing chat bubbles on the other) would best capture the comparative design, but only if it can be done with one continuous photo, not a collage.

### 2.3 Hard constraints to enforce when generating or selecting
- 1200 × 900 px PNG, color, no transparency, no whitespace borders, no text overlay, no logos, no app/brand identifiers.
- Single continuous image (no collage, no diptych composition).
- If a person's face is identifiable, written permission from the model is required at submission; safer to use a perspective that does not show identifiable faces (over-the-shoulder shot, hands-on-keyboard, side angle with face out of frame), which also removes the need for a model release.
- If generated by an AI image tool or sourced from a stockphoto site, retain the licence/proof of purchase and record source + licence for the upload form.
- Avoid the overused clichés JMIR calls out: generic "man at a computer," isolated keyboard, stethoscope-on-laptop.

### 2.4 Recommended first choice
An over-the-shoulder photograph of a medical student in clinical attire viewing a clerkship log on a laptop, with a senior clinician partially visible alongside. No identifiable faces. Real medical setting (no cartoon style). Saved as 1200 × 900 px PNG. This satisfies every technical and content rule, is on-topic for the comparative AI-vs-human-feedback theme, and avoids the cliché stockphoto pool.

### 2.5 Pre-submission checklist
- [ ] PNG, 1200 × 900 px (or at minimum 800 × 600 px), 4:3 landscape
- [ ] Full color, no monochrome regions covering the whole image
- [ ] No text, logos, watermarks, or app branding
- [ ] No collage / diptych / charts / diagrams / cartoon style
- [ ] No identifiable faces, or signed model release on file
- [ ] Source + licence recorded (own work / CC-BY / CC0 / purchased stockphoto with receipt)
- [ ] Not reused from any prior publication by the same authors
